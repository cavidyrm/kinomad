    Questions

    Before you start.

    01Do I need experience to start?

    No. Every membership includes a free onboarding session where a coach teaches the core lifts and sets your baseline. Beginners train alongside veterans every day.

    02Is there a contract?

    Never. All memberships are month-to-month. Pause or cancel anytime — no fees, no phone calls.

    03What are the staffed hours?

    Coaches are on the floor 5am–9pm weekdays and 7am–4pm weekends. Members get 24/7 keyfob access.

    04Can I freeze my membership?

    Yes — freeze for up to three months a year at no charge for travel, injury or life.
